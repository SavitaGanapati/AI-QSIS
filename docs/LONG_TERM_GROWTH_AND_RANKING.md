# Long-Term Growth, Ranking and Scenario Fields

This page describes the long-term-oriented workbook views and their shared fields.

## LongTerm Ranking

This page presents `Rank`, `Stock`, `Priority`, `Sector`, `CP`, `FuturePotentialScore`, `GrowthStage`, `RevGrowth`, `RevenueAccelerationScore`, `EarningsAccelerationScore`, `MarginExpansionScore`, `ProfitabilityTrajectoryScore`, `TechnicalScore`, `ValuationScore`, `RiskLevel`, `LongTermView`, `BullCase`, `BaseCase`, `BearCase`, and `FinalReco`.

Its role is to provide a compact prioritised list. Use the All Stocks page for a deeper look at price position, momentum, volume, fundamental metrics, concerns and warnings.

## Premarket Strength

This view combines market-bias, relative-strength, moving-average, growth, quality, valuation, technical and overall scores with long-term growth features and explanatory text. The name should only be described as genuinely premarket if the data timestamp and execution schedule support that use.

## FuturePotentialScore

Treat `FuturePotentialScore` as a system-generated ranking score. Its exact range, component weights, normalisation, missing-data treatment and relationship to other scores must be verified from implementation. Do not call it a forecast probability or expected percentage return unless it is explicitly designed and validated as such.

## GrowthStage and growth-related scores

- `GrowthStage` is a category assigned by the system; document its possible values and thresholds.
- `RevGrowth` is a financial growth metric whose period and units must be confirmed.
- `RevenueAccelerationScore` and `EarningsAccelerationScore` are score fields associated with acceleration.
- `MarginExpansionScore` relates to margin expansion.
- `ProfitabilityTrajectoryScore` represents a profitability-trajectory assessment.

The score names describe intended dimensions, but do not establish exact formulas. If the implementation uses financial statements, explain fiscal periods, data providers, reporting lags and missing values.

## Technical and valuation components

`TechnicalScore` and `ValuationScore` add market and valuation context to the long-term views. Document the actual inputs and how the system handles unprofitable companies, negative earnings, extreme ratios, missing data and sector differences. Do not assume that a single valuation metric is appropriate for every company type.

## RiskLevel, Priority and FinalReco

These are labels that summarise the system's output. Their meanings should be documented through the actual rules and thresholds. Unless empirically calibrated, a risk category should not be described as a probability of loss or guarantee of safety. A recommendation label should not replace human review.

## BullCase, BaseCase and BearCase

These fields present three scenario-style perspectives. To describe them accurately, verify whether they are generated by fixed rules, a language model, numeric forecasting, or a combination. Explain their assumptions and time horizon if available. Without such evidence, call them narrative scenarios rather than statistically calibrated forecasts or price targets.

## How to use these views together

1. Use LongTerm Ranking to identify the highest-priority names under the system's current rules.
2. Open All Stocks to inspect technical, fundamental, price-range, volume and warning fields.
3. Compare growth and quality with valuation and risk rather than relying on a single composite score.
4. Read scenario fields as conditional possibilities, not promises.
5. Independently review source financials and consider historical evaluation before relying on rankings for decisions.
